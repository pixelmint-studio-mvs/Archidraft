## 1. STATUTORY CONTEXT & PROTECTED SUBJECT MATTER

Under the **Indian Copyright Act, 1957**:
- Architectural drawings, structural plans, elevation diagrams, layout drawings, and CAD models are recognized as original **artistic works** (Section 2(c)(ii)) and **literary/technical works** (Section 2(o)).
- The creator or author is the first owner of copyright (Section 17), subject to express written contracts of service or assignment deeds complying with Section 19 of the Act.
- Section 57 protects the **moral rights** of the author, including the right to claim authorship and restrain distortion or mutilation prejudicial to professional reputation.
