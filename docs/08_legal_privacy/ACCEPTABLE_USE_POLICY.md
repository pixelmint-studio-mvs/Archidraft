## 1. PURPOSE AND PROFESSIONAL STANDARDS

Archi Draft is designed as a secure, professional coordination workspace for the architectural, engineering, and construction (AEC) community in India. To protect the integrity of professional project workflows, the confidentiality of architectural drawings, and the security of all participants, users must conduct themselves with professional diligence, mutual respect, and legal compliance at all times.

---

## 2. PROHIBITED BEHAVIORS AND ACTIVITIES

You agree that you will not engage in, facilitate, or attempt any of the following prohibited activities:

### 2.1. Unauthorized Access & Security Circumvention
- Probing, scanning, or testing the vulnerability of the Archi Draft platform, Cloudflare Workers, D1 database, or R2 storage without express written authorization.
- Circumventing, tampering with, or attempting to bypass server-side role authorization, project membership checks, or cross-user data isolation controls.
- Accessing or attempting to access project files, drawing versions, messages, or financials belonging to projects you do not own or are not formally assigned to.
- Forging HTTP headers, JWT authentication tokens, or API requests to impersonate other users or elevated administrative privileges.

### 2.2. Account Impersonation & Identity Fraud
- Creating an account using false personal, professional, or academic credentials.
- Impersonating any licensed architect, chartered engineer, structural consultant, student, draughtsman, or studio administrator.
- Misrepresenting your professional qualifications, municipal approvals, or firm affiliation.
- Permitting unauthorized third parties to use your account credentials to perform work on the platform.

### 2.3. Unauthorized Disclosure of Confidential Project Documents
- Downloading, exporting, copying, or distributing client project drawings, site plans, structural calculations, or audio notes for any purpose outside authorized project execution.
- Publishing confidential client drawings, site addresses, or project briefs on public social media, external forums, or personal marketing portfolios without the express prior written consent of the project owner.
- Disclosing proprietary building specifications or municipal approval drawings to competing commercial entities.

### 2.4. Malicious Uploads & Technical Disruption
- Uploading any drawing, script, executable, or file containing viruses, Trojan horses, worms, time bombs, keyloggers, or other malicious computer code.
- Uploading intentionally corrupted DWG, DXF, or PDF files designed to trigger memory exhaustion or server denial of service.
- Interfering with or disrupting the normal operation of the platform, servers, or connected networks.

### 2.5. Harassment, Abuse & Unprofessional Communication
- Using collaboration hub messages or recorded voice notes to harass, threaten, demean, stalk, or abuse any other user, draughtsman, student, or administrator.
- Transmitting defamatory, obscene, sexually explicit, hateful, or discriminatory communications.
- Sending unsolicited advertising, promotional materials, spam, or chain messages.

### 2.6. Automated Scraping & Bulk Extraction
- Using spiders, robots, scrapers, automated scripts, or crawler bots to harvest, extract, or index drawings, user profiles, lesson contents, or project data from the platform.
- Conducting bulk automated downloads of CAD files or platform curriculum materials.

### 2.7. Unauthorized Third-Party Intellectual Property
- Uploading architectural drawings, engineering models, survey data, or photographs without holding copyright or having obtained explicit written permission from the copyright owner.
- Removing or altering any copyright notices, watermarks, digital signatures, or proprietary legends embedded in technical drawings.

## 4. INVESTIGATION, ENFORCEMENT & REMEDIES

Archi Draft reserves the right to monitor platform usage, audit logs, and file transactions to ensure compliance with this policy and applicable law.

If we determine, in our sole reasonable discretion, that a user has violated this policy:
1. **Warning & Content Removal:** We may issue a formal warning and immediately remove offending files, messages, or comments.
2. **Assignment Revocation:** We may revoke a draughtsman's or student's assignment to an ongoing project.
3. **Suspension or Termination:** We may suspend or permanently terminate the offending user's account without refund or prior notice.
4. **Legal Action:** In cases involving severe data breaches, theft of proprietary CAD drawings, or intentional system attacks, we will pursue all available civil remedies under the Indian Contract Act and Copyright Act, and will file criminal reports under the **Information Technology Act, 2000 (Sections 43, 66, and 72A)** and the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)**.
