1. **Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Workers, D1 Database, R2 Object Storage):**

## 6. STATUTORY LOG RETENTION VS. OPERATIONAL ARCHIVES

We explicitly distinguish statutory record retention mandates from proposed operational archiving:

6.1. **Mandatory Statutory Logs:**
- Under **Direction No. 20(3)/2022-CERT-In**, system logs, access logs, and user consent records are retained for a minimum of **180 days** within Indian jurisdiction.
- Financial transaction records and invoices are maintained for **8 financial years** under the Income Tax Act, 1961 and CGST Act, 2017.

6.2. **Operational Drawing Archives (Subject to Review):**
- Retention periods for completed CAD drawings and project revision histories are currently under business review. Commissioning engineers and draughtsmen are strongly advised to maintain independent local backups of completed deliverables.

## 7. SAFEGUARDS FOR STUDENT USERS AND MINORS (<18 YEARS)

7.1. **Statutory Alignment:** Under Section 9 of the DPDP Act, 2023:
- Verifiable parental or lawful guardian consent is mandatory prior to processing personal data of individuals under 18 years of age.
- The platform strictly prohibits behavioral monitoring, tracking, or targeted advertising directed at students under 18.
- Personal data of minors will never be processed in any manner likely to cause harm to their health, well-being, or moral development.

7.2. **Current Implementation Status:** Because technical infrastructure for verifiable parental consent and parental dashboard controls requires full operational implementation, **self-service registration remains restricted to adults aged 18 and older** until formal certification of under-18 safeguards.

## 8. STATUTORY USER RIGHTS UNDER DPDP ACT, 2023

As a data principal, you hold the following rights:
1. **Right to Access Information:** You may request a summary of personal data being processed about you and the identities of data fiduciaries or processors with whom it has been shared.
2. **Right to Correction and Updating:** You may review and update inaccurate or incomplete profile details via the **Profile** section of the app.
3. **Right to Erasure:** You may request the erasure of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to statutory retention obligations.
4. **Right of Grievance Redressal:** You have the right to register grievances with our Grievance Officer and receive a formal response within statutory timelines.
5. **Right to Nominate:** You have the right to nominate an individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
