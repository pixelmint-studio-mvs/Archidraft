> ### POLICY NOTICE
> This Subscription, Cancellation and Refund Policy sets forth the terms governing project order cancellations, milestone billing adjustments, course fee refunds, and subscription cancellations for services rendered through Archi Draft.
>
> All refunds are administered equitably based on verified drafting labor performed, platform infrastructure consumed, and educational module progression.

---

## 1. DRAFTING PROJECT MILESTONE CANCELLATION

When a Client / Consulting Engineer commissions drafting deliverables through Archi Draft, work is structured around verifiable project milestones. Cancellation eligibility depends on the stage of execution at the time written notice is received:

### 1.1. Pre-Assignment Cancellation (Stage 1)
- **Status:** Brief submitted, but no Draughtsman has been formally assigned, or work has not commenced.
- **Refund Eligibility:** **100% refund** of the deposited milestone fee (less third-party payment gateway transaction fees if incurred).

### 1.2. In-Progress Drafting Cancellation (Stage 2)
- **Status:** A Draughtsman has been assigned and technical drafting is actively in progress, but no initial drawing version has yet been uploaded.
- **Refund Eligibility:** **Pro-rata refund**. The Studio Administrator will assess the hours expended and preliminary CAD work completed. The client will be refunded the remaining unexpended balance of the milestone fee, and all preliminary work files completed up to that point will be delivered to the client.

### 1.3. Post-Delivery Milestone Cancellation (Stage 3)
- **Status:** Initial drawing deliverables (`.dwg`, `.pdf`) have been uploaded to the project hub for client review.
- **Refund Eligibility:** **Non-refundable**. Milestone fees for completed and delivered drafting packages cannot be refunded once files have been accessed or downloaded.
- However, the client retains the right to cancel any subsequent, uncommenced project milestones without penalty.

## 2. STUDENT COURSE ENROLLMENT AND TUITION REFUNDS

For learners and institutions enrolled in vocational CAD courses or technical drafting modules through the Student Portal:

2.1. **7-Day Grace Window:** A student may request a full cancellation and refund within **seven (7) calendar days** of course purchase, provided that:
- Total course progression is **under 15%** of the curriculum; and
- No practical assignments have been graded; and
- No completion credential or verification certificate has been issued.

2.2. **Non-Refundable Circumstances:** Course fees become strictly non-refundable if:
- More than seven (7) days have elapsed since enrollment; or
- Course completion exceeds 15%; or
- A verified certificate of completion has been generated; or
- The student's account has been suspended for academic dishonesty or unauthorized sharing of course materials.

## 3. PLATFORM SUBSCRIPTIONS (WHEN ACTIVATED)

3.1. **Recurring Billing:** Where recurring monthly or annual platform subscriptions are activated for enhanced cloud storage or enterprise team features:
- Subscriptions may be canceled at any time via account settings.
- Cancellation will take effect at the conclusion of the current prepaid billing period.
- We do not offer retroactive pro-rated refunds for partially used subscription months.

---

## 4. REFUND PROCESSING AND TIMELINES

4.1. **Submission:** All cancellation and refund requests must be submitted in writing to `support@draughtsmanstudio.com` with the project ID, transaction receipt, and justification.

4.2. **Evaluation:** The Studio Operations Administrator will review the project timeline and audit logs and issue a determination within **three (3) business days**.

4.3. **Disbursement:** Approved refunds will be credited back to the original payment method through our certified payment processor within **seven (7) to ten (10) business days**, depending on banking institution processing cycles.
