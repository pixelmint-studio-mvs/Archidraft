> ### POLICY OVERVIEW
> This Policy establishes clear operational standards, correction parameters, and billing boundaries for drafting revisions, annotations, and dimensional corrections conducted through Archi Draft.
> Our objective is to ensure high drafting precision while maintaining equitable boundaries between included correction rounds and new design scope.

---

## 1. STANDARD INCLUDED REVISION ROUNDS

1.1. **Two (2) Revision Cycles:** Every standard drafting commission submitted through Archi Draft includes **two (2) complete revision rounds** at no additional charge.

1.2. **Consolidation Requirement:** To maintain project velocity, the commissioning Client / Consulting Engineer must provide consolidated, comprehensive feedback for each round rather than piecemeal ad-hoc correction requests.

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF IN-SCOPE CORRECTIONS

The two included revision rounds apply strictly to corrections arising within the parameters of the original project brief:

2.1. **Drafting Errors:** Rectification of misaligned CAD lines, incorrect layer assignments, missing title block details, or discrepancies between floor plans and section cuts.

2.2. **Minor Dimensional Adjustments:** Minor adjustments to door/window openings, wall offsets, or internal room partition dimensions that do not impact structural load-bearing elements or municipal setbacks.

2.3. **Annotation and Styling Changes:** Updates to text annotations, dimension styles, hatching patterns, door/window schedules, and municipal key legends.

---

## 3. SCOPE EXPANSIONS (OUT-OF-SCOPE REVISIONS)

Revisions that exceed the original project brief constitute **Scope Expansions** and are not covered by the included revision rounds:

3.1. **Structural and Architectural Redesign:**
- Altering the fundamental structural system (e.g., changing from load-bearing masonry to framed RCC structure);
- Modifying column grids, shear wall configurations, or stairwell locations;
- Adding additional storeys, mezzanines, basements, or increasing gross built-up area;
- Modifying plot dimensions, site setbacks, or orientation.

3.2. **Complete Conceptual Rework:** Scrapping approved conceptual layouts in favor of an entirely new architectural scheme.

3.3. **Commercial Terms for Scope Expansions:** Work involving scope expansions will be quoted as an independent project addendum or billed on an agreed hourly/area-based drafting rate.

---

## 4. TIMELINES AND SUBMISSION WINDOWS

4.1. **Client Review Window:** Upon delivery of a drafting milestone or drawing version, the Client / Engineer has **seven (7) calendar days** to inspect the files and submit consolidated revision requests through the platform hub.

4.2. **Deemed Acceptance:** If no correction requests or messages are submitted within fourteen (14) calendar days of deliverable upload, the milestone is deemed accepted, and the revision round is closed.

4.3. **Draughtsman Turnaround:** Standard in-scope revision iterations are targeted for completion within **48 to 72 business hours** of receiving consolidated instructions, subject to project complexity.

---

## 5. ESCALATION AND ARBITRATION OF DISPUTES

Where a disagreement arises as to whether a requested revision constitutes an in-scope correction or a billable scope expansion:
1. The project message thread and original brief will be evaluated by the **Studio Operations Administrator**;
2. The Studio Administrator will render an objective technical assessment within two (2) business days;
3. If still unresolved, the matter may be escalated to the Proprietor at `support@draughtsmanstudio.com`.
