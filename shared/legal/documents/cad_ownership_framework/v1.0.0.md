> ### CONTRACTUAL NOTICE ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
> This Framework establishes the legal and contractual rules governing intellectual property (IP), copyright allocation, file licensing, and confidentiality for architectural briefs, computer-aided design (CAD) drawings, Building Information Modeling (BIM) files, and technical documentation uploaded or produced on Archi Draft.
>
> **NO AUTOMATIC TRANSFER OF OWNERSHIP OCCURS MERELY BY UPLOADING OR PROCESSING DRAWINGS ON ARCHI DRAFT.**

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1. **Background IP:** Pre-existing architectural designs, survey layouts, sketches, engineering calculations, CAD templates, and proprietary specifications created or owned by a User prior to uploading to Archi Draft.

1.2. **Foreground Deliverables:** Technical 2D drafting files, 3D models, approval sheets, and working drawings produced by assigned draughtsmen pursuant to a specific project brief submitted through the platform.

1.3. **Platform Assets:** Proprietary software code, UI designs, Pixmint Bot models, educational curricula, lesson modules, and training rubrics owned by DRAUGHTSMAN STUDIO.

---

## 2. BACKGROUND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

2.1. **Client / Engineer Ownership:** The commissioning engineer or client retains all right, title, and interest (including copyright under Section 17 of the **Indian Copyright Act, 1957**) in all Background IP uploaded to the platform.

2.2. **No Claim of Platform Ownership:** DRAUGHTSMAN STUDIO makes no claim of ownership over your proprietary architectural concepts, site plans, or client designs.

---

## 3. LIMITED OPERATIONAL PLATFORM LICENSE

3.1. **Scope of License:** By uploading Background IP and project files to Archi Draft, you grant DRAUGHTSMAN STUDIO a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, limited license strictly for the duration of the project to:
- Store and cache files within **Cloudflare R2 Object Storage**;
- Generate technical previews, thumbnails, and PDF rendering layers;
- Transmit files to designated and assigned project collaborators (draughtsmen, studio administrators, and assigned students);
- Execute automated file-format validations and anti-malware security scans.

3.2. **Revocability:** This operational license terminates automatically upon deletion of the project files or complete account termination, subject to mandatory statutory audit logs.

---

## 4. ALLOCATION OF FOREGROUND DELIVERABLES

4.1. **No Automatic Assignment:** Producing or delivering drawings on Archi Draft does **not** automatically execute a copyright assignment. Under Section 19 of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, any assignment of copyright must be in writing, specifying the rights assigned, the duration, territorial extent, and consideration.

4.2. **Transfer Upon Full Payment:**
- Ownership or exclusive licensing of final completed Foreground Deliverables transfers to the commissioning Client / Engineer **only upon full settlement of all agreed project milestone fees**.
- Prior to full milestone payment, the commissioning user receives a revocable, non-exclusive license to inspect the deliverables for revision and approval purposes only.

---

## 5. DRAUGHTSMAN OBLIGATIONS AND CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1. **Confidentiality:** Draughtsmen acknowledge that all client briefs, site locations, and drawing files are confidential trade information.

5.2. **Prohibition of Third-Party Reuse:** Draughtsmen are strictly prohibited from:
- Re-selling, sharing, or re-using client CAD files or drawing layers for any other project;
- Publishing client drawings in personal public portfolios, social media, or marketing channels without express prior written consent from the commissioning client and Draughtsman Studio.

6.2. **Curriculum IP:** All lesson videos, practice drafting assignments, course materials, and technical rubrics provided by Draughtsman Studio remain the exclusive intellectual property of DRAUGHTSMAN STUDIO. Students are granted a personal, non-transferable, non-commercial educational license only.
